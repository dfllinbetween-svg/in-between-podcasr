About This Episode

In this episode, we sit down with Edward Nolan and Laurent Cases, two scholars of Latin and Greek Classics at NTU's Department of Foreign Languages and Literatures. Together, they discuss the study of Classics, the experience of pursuing graduate research, and the place of classical languages in contemporary academia.
